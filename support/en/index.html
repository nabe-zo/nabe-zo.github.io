Random Drum Phrases Support

Drum phrase practice app

Getting Started

  1. Choose the number of beats per bar, number of bars, note values, difficulty, rests, and instruments.
  2. Tap “Generate Phrase.”
  3. Tap a note in the score to change its accent.
  4. Set the tempo, choose whether to use a count-in, and tap “Play.”
  5. Give your favorite phrases names and save them.

If You Cannot Hear the Audio

Check your device’s volume and where your Bluetooth devices or headphones are connected. Playback stops if you switch to another app while a phrase is playing.

Restoring Your Purchase

To restore your purchase for removing ads, open the “Remove Ads” screen in the app and tap “Restore Purchases.”
