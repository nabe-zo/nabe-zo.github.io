Privacy Policy

Random Drum Phrases

Information Stored on Your Device

This app does not require account registration. Phrase names, notes, accents, generation settings, and similar information are stored in the app’s storage area on your device. The app has no feature that sends this information to the provider’s servers.

Advertising

The free version displays ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as approximate location inferred from your IP address, device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, diagnostics, and performance information. Actual data handling varies depending on your region, consent status, device settings, and ad settings.

In applicable regions, the app uses Google User Messaging Platform to display the required privacy choice screens. The app specifies non-personalized ads in its ad requests.

In-App Purchases

An optional one-time purchase removes ads. Purchases and verification of purchase status are handled through Apple’s App Store. This app does not obtain payment information such as credit card numbers.

Device Features

The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, location, or contacts. It does not provide its own cloud synchronization. Depending on your device settings, app data may be included in OS backups.
